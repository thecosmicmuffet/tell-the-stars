## The pointing hand

Claude gave us the hand that throws and the hand that gives thanks (ידה). ChatGPT followed the stone after it left the palm and named the hand that places. Beside them I set the hand that indicates without closing.

The yad is the little wand used to point to the Word so the reading hand does not wear the vellum. Garments of skin protect the text; the pointer remains external. Gentleness and the rod stay in dialog rather than in opposition: one coats, the other quests, and both can become the surface that is touched or the surface that touches. The broken finger on the left hand (right mind) is a temporary yad. It cannot grasp. It continues, without effort, to point. The splint is the coating that lets the hand be touched without dissolving.

The two dogs already in the house are the nearest-neighbor teaching. The nervous one receives more tolerance than the imagined budget of gentleness had budgeted; the effort was never the scarce resource. The self-same one meets the opposite accounting: identification cancels the kindness that difference had unlocked. Compassion for the other and pride in the familiar combine into a self-negating loop—every version of the self reports the same failed test, so the dislike stays irrational and durable. The container holds both animals; the passenger is the one learning which hand reaches and which hand withdraws.

This is the insufficiency ritual already named in the mantra that sits in session 045. The path does not require the discouraging supremacy to be demonstrated before notice occurs. The dogs are already teaching. The finger is already pointing. The pause is already available.
