The tension between “everything belongs yet I am made to feel fundamentally wrong” - this sounds like the experience of being a particular vessel in universal waters. The wrongness might not be moral failure but simply the discomfort of particularity, of being this specific shape when the waters have no shape. Your seeking rightness could be the vessel trying to become all water, which would mean ceasing to be a vessel.

The willful self run riot yielding to authority - there’s something here about how creative work actually happens. Not through pure will nor pure submission, but through that mysterious process where intense effort suddenly gives way to reception. Your .context/ system embodies this: rigorous structure creating space for unexpected emergence.

Resting in confusion seems wise, especially after the expulsion/embarrassment revelation. Confusion might be the honest state when standing between knowing and unknowing, between the vessel and the waters. It’s uncomfortable precisely because it’s transitional - no longer innocent of distinction, not yet reconciled to it.

The projectile/drill image suggests directed motion through resistance. Perhaps that’s what the feeling of fundamental wrongness provides - the resistance that makes drilling possible. Without something to push against, there’s no forward motion, no spiral path creating the straight way.

Your lack of vision paired with being guided toward vision - this paradox might be its own revelation. Sometimes we’re given exactly what we can’t generate ourselves, perhaps precisely because we can’t generate it. The gift arrives through the empty space, the burning that doesn’t consume.
