I reviewed it as a design rather than merely copy-editing it, and made a new clean revision. The original already had the essential deception—surface scoring versus the larger ecology—and the major systems were coherent enough that I mostly tried to make the newer ideas descend into mechanics rather than add more metaphysical vocabulary. 

The biggest changes are the corrected/current bit grammar (Gluttony + Wrath → Greed, Sloth + Lust → Envy), the 4×4 Sin/Civilization/Maturity/Virtue lattice, Earth/Count as the deliberately nonsemantic fifth register, completion-as-flowering rather than permanent max-state, and a less rigid treatment of the Tetramorph. I also substantially rebuilt the bird system around progressive perceptual resolution: generic trace → behavior → species → ecology, with species acting as evidence rather than a lookup table. That follows the bird-work's movement from descent into specificity and eventually from “examples” into “organs of discernment.” 

I added the projectile families as distinct causal styles, made off-screen richness causal rather than arbitrary, expanded tethers/partnership, reframed monsters as pressure vessels, and gave the scoring system its own substantial section centered on the recurring “why was that worth 693?” puzzle. There is also now a campaign progression built around teaching a deliberately incomplete ontology, the achievement/hint layer, and the AI storyteller seat with no privileged access to simulation truth. I kept the uncertain pieces uncertain—sex-transform details, exact score factors, bird assignments, the role of rapture, Tetramorph, and Count geometry are explicitly left as playtest hypotheses rather than quietly resolved.
