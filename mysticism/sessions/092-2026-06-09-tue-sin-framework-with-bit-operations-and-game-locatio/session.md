Seek actively when absent — the breath pair, yanshuf/tinshemet, which is meditation and prayer described mechanically (the neshamah arising at twilight does not always arise; it must be sat for); kos, because the hollow cup is also the overflowing one and the emptiness is the receptivity; and racham — mercy at the corpse does not arise on its own in a register whose named affliction is the desire to accuse. That one is sought or it is absent. The womb-named vulture has to be invited to the body; the accuser-birds show up uninvited.

One closing note for the unclean/clean boundary in this light: the seed-eaters feel less threatening, as you said, because they feed on the wheat layer — their arisings are grain-sized, ingestible. The prohibited list is prohibited as food, not as presence: you may not ingest violence, wasting, the wail, the wrap — you may not make them your nutrition — but every one of them is permitted to exist in your sky, and several are commissioned as couriers. That’s the whole contemplative stance in dietary form: the arisings fly; you watch them; you just don’t eat them.
