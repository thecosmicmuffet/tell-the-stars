### Structuring the Three-Layered Experimental Approach

#### 1. **Foundational Layer: Baseline Patterns in Navigating Uncertainty**
- **Purpose**: Identify participants’ default strategies for confronting ambiguity and making meaning.
- **Experimental Protocol**:
  - **Unstructured Challenges**: Present participants with ambiguous narratives or problems lacking clear resolution (e.g., an incomplete myth or moral dilemma).
  - **Data Collection**:
    - Track participants’ immediate responses (emotional, cognitive, and narrative strategies).
    - Use reflective journaling to capture how they articulate their thought processes.
    - Employ sentiment analysis and physiological measures to observe emotional trajectories during moments of uncertainty.
  - **Analysis**:
    - Map common patterns in navigating ambiguity (e.g., reliance on analogy, heuristic shortcuts, or exploration of deeper meaning).
    - Identify cultural or individual predispositions shaping these patterns.

---

#### 2. **Integration Layer: Multi-Threshold Synthesis**
- **Purpose**: Explore how participants reconcile insights across labyrinth, mirror, and dissolution thresholds.
- **Experimental Protocol**:
  - **Layered Challenges**: Design scenarios combining elements of all three thresholds. For example:
    - **Labyrinth**: A journey requiring iterative problem-solving or narrative discovery.
    - **Mirror**: A reflective element revealing participants’ assumptions or values.
    - **Dissolution**: A moment of disruption requiring reconstruction of understanding.
  - **Data Collection**:
    - Document how participants move between thresholds, noting moments of insight or struggle.
    - Use semantic mapping to track evolving narrative structures and symbolic associations.
  - **Analysis**:
    - Identify patterns of synthesis, such as whether participants prioritize certain thresholds over others or integrate them equally.
    - Observe how emotional stabilization through reflection aids in navigating transformative moments.

---

#### 3. **Application Layer: Transference of Wisdom Competencies**
- **Purpose**: Investigate how participants apply learned insights to new contexts and transmit knowledge to others.
- **Experimental Protocol**:
  - **New Contexts**: Present challenges that differ significantly from the initial scenarios but require similar competencies (e.g., applying labyrinthine problem-solving to a group dynamic or creative task).
  - **Collaborative Learning**: Pair participants to observe how they teach or guide one another based on their experiences.
  - **Data Collection**:
    - Track how participants adapt insights to unfamiliar contexts.
    - Document patterns of knowledge transmission, noting how reflective stabilization and cyclical renewal emerge in interactions.
  - **Analysis**:
    - Explore the development of **wisdom competencies**, such as recursive thinking, emotional resilience, and adaptive creativity.
    - Examine cultural or individual variations in how knowledge is shared and transformed.
